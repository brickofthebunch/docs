---
title: "Packing and Shipping Orders in BrickDeck"
sidebarTitle: "Overview"
description: "Pack Mode moves picked items to the bench so you can produce labels and postage without leaving BrickDeck as you finish each order."
---

The packing bench is where picked quantities arrive ready for bagging, labelling and dispatch. Pack Mode carries your picks straight through to postage and label printing so you can finish orders without switching tools.

The full shipping workflow is still in active development. Packing guides will arrive here once the features are live.
